=== STOP THE APP ===
If it is running in the foreground terminal, press Ctrl+C.

If it is still running in the background, find it first:
Get-Process | Where-Object { $_.ProcessName -match 'python|ffmpeg|yt-dlp|youtube' } | Select-Object Id, ProcessName, Path

Then stop the specific PID:
Stop-Process -Id <PID> -Force

Or nuke everything matching by name (use with care - kills ALL python/ffmpeg processes, not just this app):
taskkill /F /IM python.exe;
taskkill /F /IM ffmpeg.exe;
